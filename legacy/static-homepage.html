딥러닝·컴퓨터 비전·수학·알고리즘·논문 리뷰에 대한 글을 꾸준히 기록하고 있습니다.

Hello, I'm Gwanghee Lee.

I'm currently a Ph.D. candidate researching Artificial Intelligence. Before my research career, I built a wide range of software — from deep-learning systems and computer vision to web & mobile apps and blockchain modules.

I regularly write about deep learning, computer vision, mathematics, algorithms, and paper reviews.
